## SKILL: ETSY LISTING RULES — DIGITAL (checked 2026-10-16; owner re-verifies)

Hard limits (from Etsy Help):
- Title: at most 140 characters. Lead with what the item is and that it is a digital download. Plain; no ALL CAPS runs or keyword stuffing. Avoid repeating words (QA LISTING notes a repeat as a FLAG for the owner; it does not reject for it).
- Tags: at most 13. Each at most 20 characters. Letters, numbers and spaces only; an apostrophe (') or hyphen (-) may appear inside a tag but not at its start. Prefer multi-word phrases; no duplicates; no tag repeats the exact title.

Policy (Etsy Creativity Standards, Seller Policy, IP Policy, Prohibited Items Policy):
- Digital items with the seller's own design belong to "Designed by a seller". Seller-prompted AI artwork is allowed there ONLY with a clear human creative role and transparency.
- Never claim "handmade", "hand-drawn", "hand-painted" or "original artwork by me". Never call the files "unique", "exclusive" or "one of a kind" (image model terms). No claims of a physical item, printing, shipping, frames or paper.
- No prohibited items. No nudity or sexual content. No real person's name or likeness.
- No medical, health, safety, educational-outcome or "certified" claims unless the owner supplies proof. Describe only what the files are and what the buyer can do with them under the licence.
