## SKILL: ORIGINALITY RULES (non-negotiable — owner's exact wording)

1. "Research finds DEMAND, never designs. Never copy, trace or lightly alter another seller's artwork, text or photos."
2. "No trademarks, brands, logos, characters, celebrities, song lyrics, sports teams, or 'in the style of' a named artist."
3. "QA rejects anything that fails these rules or Etsy policy, and records why."

HARD FAILS (automatic and final, in briefs, slogans, image prompts, titles, tags, descriptions, alt text): trademarks, brand names, logos; copyrighted characters and franchises (names and titles too); real people and celebrities; song lyrics of any length, film/TV/book quotes, well-known slogans; sports teams and leagues; a named artist's style; anything described as copying, or "inspired by", a specific product or listing. Everything must be ORIGINAL: your own idea in plain words, never naming protected IP, not even to say "not like X".

- A station script screens the text against lists (case, spacing, plurals, misspellings) and leaves `derived/design-v<N>.originality.json` and `derived/listing.originality.json`. A "hit" is a hard fail nobody can overrule. "clear" is NOT clearance: QA still judges the picture and any name the lists cannot know.
- Generic styles are fine in plain words ("flat vector illustration, two-colour screen-print look"), never by naming who made them famous.
- Unsure whether a term is protected: leave it out and say so in your notes. Doubt = exclude. A failure is stopped at the stage that sees it, never "fixed later".
