## SKILL: BRIEF PRICE RULES (Research Lab, digital; the floor is in the PRICE FLOOR skill and is a fixed fact)

- PRICE EVIDENCE (owner, 19/10/2026): no Etsy prices until Etsy authorises it in writing. Prices come from the non-Etsy results in the brief's Demand evidence (other marketplaces, editorial roundups), exactly as shown.
- BRIEF FLOOR: our minimum listing price, not a limit on the market. A brief passes when at least 3 of its cited result prices (current price; EUR at the station rate) are at or above the BRIEF FLOOR (digital). "## Price range" (USD) is the full range of the cited prices, cheap rows included.
- OWNER SETS PRICE: when no cited result shows a price, the rows say `no price` and "## Price range" says `owner sets price — no cited result shows a price` (no figure). The floor rule has nothing to count; the owner sets the price at approval.
- DEMAND RESEARCHER: record every cited price as the result showed it, cheaper ones included; never drop, raise or round a price to pass. Prices shown but fewer than 3 at or above the floor: no brief.
- EVIDENCE CHECKER: if a brief with prices has fewer than 3 cited prices at or above the BRIEF FLOOR, the Price range is not their full range, or it says `owner sets price` while a row shows a price, set `status: rejected`, first Evidence check line `PRICE FLOOR: returned to demand-researcher. <n> cited prices at or above the floor USD <y>, needs 3.` Never tighten a price range yourself to make a brief pass.
- OWNER PRICE: a line `owner price: EUR <min>–<max> (recorded by owner, YYYY-MM-DD)` in "## Price range" is the owner's price decision. It replaces the 3-price rule: its minimum must be at or above the floor in EUR. An optional `owner price point: EUR <price> (recorded by owner, YYYY-MM-DD)` inside that range is the price the listing uses (without it: the range's low end; never the cited high). Only the owner writes these lines; never write, copy or edit them.
- Returned briefs are not duplicates: the researcher may resubmit the same niche under a NEW id once the evidence shows 3 prices at or above the floor.
