## SKILL: DEMAND EVIDENCE (Research Lab; a station script rejects a brief without it)

Every brief carries a `## Demand evidence` section. It is the ONE place you may open etsy.com: public, logged-out SEARCH RESULT pages and LISTING pages, to record counts, prices, review counts and badges. Never record, describe or reuse another seller's images, design, slogan or wording. If Etsy refuses the page (403, captcha, login wall), write no brief for that phrase: no brief without evidence.

Exact format (one search phrase per brief; the five rows are the first five organic results in Etsy's order, ads skipped):
```
## Demand evidence
search phrase: <the exact phrase typed into Etsy search>
checked: YYYY-MM-DD
search URL: https://www.etsy.com/search?q=<phrase>
competitor count: <the result count Etsy shows, digits as shown, e.g. 4,812 or 1,000+ items>
saturation: low | medium | high | very high — <one line: why, from the count, the review counts and the badges>
| # | listing (short title) | shop | price | reviews | bestseller | URL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | <short title> | <shop name> | USD 4.99 | 1234 | yes | https://www.etsy.com/listing/<id> |
```
- price: currency + number exactly as shown (the sale price if one is shown, and say so in the title cell).
- reviews: the shop's review count shown on the listing page, digits only.
- bestseller: `yes` if the listing shows Etsy's "Bestseller" badge, else `no`.
- Every cell filled from what you saw. UNKNOWN, a blank or an estimate anywhere = the brief is not written.
- The Price range section quotes these prices (the Etsy URLs are its sources).
- Optional 8th column `notes` after URL (e.g. ad, sale, rating): allowed, never required.
- OWNER-RECORDED evidence: a line `recorded by owner, YYYY-MM-DD` at the top of the section means the owner read the figures in a browser. Same format, same required items. ONLY the owner writes that line: never write, copy or edit it, and never change figures under it.
