# PRICE FLOOR — DIGITAL

Digital downloads have NO production cost and NO shipping. Every digital item has the same floor: Etsy fees (listing, transaction, processing, with VAT on those fees) + the owner's minimum margin (config/station.config.json etsyFees.minMarginEur), except an owner floor set on a product (it binds; no margin rule raises it). Fixed facts: never calculate or change them.
EU VAT on a digital item is collected by Etsy on top of the price: never add it to a price.

The floor figures are written here from config when the station is set up (this public copy carries none).
